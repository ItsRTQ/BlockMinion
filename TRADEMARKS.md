# Trademarks and branding

This document explains how the project's own names may be used. It is not a claim of registered trademark status:
as of November 2026, **"BlockMinion" and "craftctl" are unregistered names used by ItsRTQ (HCoding)**, and a
clearance search has not been performed. Registration, and the enforceability of what follows, are matters for a lawyer.

## Official Marks

- **BlockMinion** — the project name (formerly "Minecraft Mod Orchestra").
- **craftctl** — the name of the command-line tool.
- The project's logo: the BlockMinion mark (`src/mod_orchestra/static/blockminion.png` and the favicons derived from it).

The `LICENSE` grants rights to the software, not to these marks (section 7).

## What you may do

- Say, truthfully, that your software, fork, service, tutorial or mod is **"based on BlockMinion"**, **"a fork of
  BlockMinion"**, **"built with BlockMinion"** or **"compatible with craftctl"**.
- Keep the names inside the code and files you redistribute where removing them would break the software or the
  required notices (`LICENSE`, `NOTICE.md`).
- Use the names to refer to the project in writing, teaching, reviews and comparisons.
- Offer paid installation, configuration, customization, training or support for BlockMinion and describe it as such.

## What you may not do

- Name your fork, product or service **"BlockMinion"**, or something confusingly similar, or present it as the official
  project, an official release, or as maintained by the project's authors.
- Use the Official Marks or logo as the primary name or identity of a Derivative Version. Give your version its own
  name and state the relationship ("Based on BlockMinion" is enough).
- Claim, or let your presentation imply, that you are the original author of BlockMinion, or that the project endorses
  you, your fork or your services.
- Use the marks in a way that suggests the project is affiliated with Mojang, Microsoft, Fabric, Forge or NeoForge.

## For forks, in practice

1. Pick your own name. Keep "Based on BlockMinion (ItsRTQ/HCoding)" visible in your README and your distribution.
2. Keep `LICENSE`, `legal/MIT.txt` and `NOTICE.md`, and add your own copyright line to the parts you wrote.
3. State that your version is modified and not the official project, and where the official project lives.
4. Do not reuse the project's logo as your own.

## Third-party names

"Minecraft" is a trademark of Mojang Synergies AB / Microsoft, and the project follows the Minecraft brand and asset
usage guidelines: it does not use "Minecraft" as part of its product name, does not ship Minecraft assets, and
describes itself only as a tool **for** Minecraft Java Edition modding. It is not affiliated with Mojang or Microsoft.
"Fabric", "Forge" and "NeoForge" are the names of independent mod loader projects; BlockMinion currently generates
Fabric mods and is affiliated with none of them.
